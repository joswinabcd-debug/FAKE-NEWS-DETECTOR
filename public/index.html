Architecture: TextCNN
Multi-Kernel Convolutional Network
Applies parallel 1D convolutional filter banks of sizes 3, 4, and 5 over word embeddings to detect salient n-gram patterns, sensationalist rhetorical tropes, and informative local phrases.
Architecture: LSTM
Sequential Context Network
Processes word sequences recurrently through specialized gating mechanisms to capture long-range narrative context, sequential dependencies, and stylistic consistency across the article.
